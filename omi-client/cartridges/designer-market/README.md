# Designer Market

Private marketing and sales-assistance cartridge for a family designer clothing business.

## Purpose

Designer Market translates a finished or in-progress clothing project into practical marketing assets without requiring the user to understand marketing software or jargon.

The clothing studio remains the source of truth for garment specifications, fabrics, measurements, tech packs, manufacturing notes, and costs. Designer Market consumes only explicitly shared project data and produces marketing/sales artifacts.

## Voice-first workflow

Example requests:

- "Help me sell this jacket."
- "Make me a product page for this coat."
- "What should I charge for this?"
- "Build a launch plan for this collection."
- "Make a wholesale sheet for buyers."
- "Write three social posts for this garment."
- "Turn this into a lookbook prompt."
- "What should I do next?"

## Initial capabilities

- product naming and positioning
- price / margin sanity check
- product-page copy
- short and long product descriptions
- materials / care / size facts
- lookbook and render prompts
- social-post drafts
- launch checklist
- customer FAQ
- wholesale line sheet text
- buyer outreach drafts
- simple campaign calendar
- customer inquiry triage
- sales / inquiry notes
- prompt history tied to the clothing project

## Guardrails

- no false claims about fabric, origin, sustainability, certifications, or manufacturing
- no invented customer testimonials or sales numbers
- no hidden ad tracking
- no automatic publishing without explicit user approval
- no automatic spending on ads
- private clothing/project data is shared only when explicitly granted
- marketing output is advisory; pricing should preserve the underlying cost/margin record

## User experience rule

The user should not have to learn marketing terminology. The assistant explains any necessary term in plain language and always provides a clear next action.
